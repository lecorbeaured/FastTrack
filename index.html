Absolutely not. This course is specifically built for people with zero credit history. That's the whole point — we start at the very beginning and walk you through everything sequentially.
How long will it take to see results? +
Most students see their first credit score appear within 30–60 days of opening their first account. Students who follow the full 90-day action plan typically reach the 640–700 range by the end. Results vary based on your starting point and how consistently you follow the plan.
Will building credit cost me money? +
The strategies in this course are designed to cost as little as possible. A secured credit card may require a $200 deposit (which you get back). Many of the best starter cards have no annual fee. Used correctly, a credit card costs you nothing if you pay the balance in full each month.

No. While the course is written with young beginners in mind, the strategies work for anyone with a thin or no credit file — including people in their 20s, 30s, or beyond who never built credit or had a negative event reset their file.
