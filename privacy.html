4 Cookies & Tracking Technologies

Cookies are small text files stored on your device by your browser. Our site uses the following types:

  • Functional cookies: Used to remember course access status after payment (stored in sessionStorage — cleared when you close the browser)
  • Third-party cookies: Set by Google Fonts, Stripe, and affiliate networks when you interact with those elements. These are governed by the respective third party's cookie policy.
  • Analytics cookies: If we implement analytics tools, these may set cookies to track aggregate traffic patterns. No personally identifiable information is shared with analytics providers.

You can control or disable cookies through your browser settings. Note that disabling cookies may affect site functionality, including course access after payment.
