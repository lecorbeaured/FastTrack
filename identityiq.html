Why It Matters

Why You Need All 3 Bureau Scores — Not Just One

Here's something most people don't realize: different lenders check different bureaus. A mortgage lender might pull all three and use the middle score. A car dealership might only check Experian. A credit card issuer might only use TransUnion.

If you're only tracking one bureau, you're flying blind on the other two — and that's exactly where you might get surprised when you apply for something important.

💡

Real example for credit repair students: When you dispute a negative item, the three bureaus process it independently. Equifax might remove it in week 2, while Experian and TransUnion take until week 5. Without 3-bureau monitoring, you don't know which disputes landed and which need follow-up.

IdentityIQ uses FICO Score 8 — the same model used by the majority of lenders when evaluating credit applications. This is more useful than VantageScore (used by Credit Karma) because it more closely reflects what lenders actually see.
How often does IdentityIQ update my scores? +
Daily. Each of your three bureau scores refreshes every 24 hours inside the dashboard. This is significantly more frequent than most competitors — Credit Karma updates weekly, and Experian's free tier updates monthly.

For casual monitoring, Credit Karma is fine. But Credit Karma doesn't show your Experian score, uses VantageScore instead of FICO, and updates weekly. If you're actively building or repairing credit and making decisions based on your scores, IdentityIQ gives you the real data you need.
Does signing up for IdentityIQ hurt my credit? +
No. Checking your own credit through IdentityIQ is a soft inquiry — it has zero impact on your scores. Only hard inquiries from lenders affect your credit.
